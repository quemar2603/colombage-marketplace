Rounded white surface with a gentle shadow — wrap any grouped content in it.

```jsx
<Card tone="white" interactive>
  <h3>Aide aux courses</h3>
  <p>Un accompagnement chaque semaine.</p>
</Card>
```

Tones: `white` (default), `cream`, `accent` (pink wash), `info` (periwinkle wash). Pass `interactive` for clickable cards (hover lift). Radius is `--radius-card` (20px); never sharp corners.
