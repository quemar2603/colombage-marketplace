Round avatar for caregivers and clients; shows a photo or initials on a soft tint.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Marie Lefèvre" tone="pink" online />
<Avatar src="/photo.jpg" name="Auxiliaire" size={72} />
```

`size` is the diameter in px. Without `src` it renders up-to-two initials. `online` adds a green availability dot. Tones: `peri`, `pink`, `navy`.
